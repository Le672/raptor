# F1 driver-profile typefaces

Verified on 2026-10-05 against https://www.formula1.com/en/drivers/max-verstappen
and the public stylesheet https://www.formula1.com/s/fonts.css.

- `northwell-clean-alt.woff2`: Northwell Clean Alt, the first-name lettering in the official driver profile. It is a handwriting typeface, not Max Verstappen's personal autograph.
- `formula1-black.woff2`: Formula1 Black, used for the uppercase family name in the same profile.
- `formula1-bold.woff2`: Formula1 Bold, used for supporting team names here.

Original URLs are recorded in `../brands/2026/sources.json`. Original ownership and font rights remain with their respective owners; no new font license is asserted by this project.
